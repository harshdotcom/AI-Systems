Reading

Papers

What each one found, in plain words

  1. 2017 Attention Is All You Need Vaswani and others, Google A model can drop recurrence and convolution and rely on attention alone. Because it reads every word of a sentence at once instead of one after another, it trains much faster, and it translated better than the best systems of its time. This is the Transformer, the design behind today’s language models. arXiv
  2. 2020 Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks Lewis and others, Facebook AI Research A language model answers more accurately when it first looks things up. Pairing a generator with a retriever over an index of documents gave answers that were more specific and more factual than the model’s memory alone, and the knowledge can be updated by replacing the index instead of retraining. arXiv
  3. 2021 LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models Hu and others, Microsoft You do not have to retrain a whole model to specialise it. Freezing the original weights and training two small matrices beside them cut the trainable parameters by up to 10,000 times and GPU memory by about three times on GPT-3, matched full fine-tuning in quality, and added no delay at inference. arXiv
