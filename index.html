About the library

Most technical writing is either a toy tutorial or a lecture on theory. These books are written for the part in between, where a system has to run in production and somebody has to answer for it.

So the chapters stay close to real constraints: KV cache budgets, consensus, query plans, context windows, guardrails. Product discovery, team leadership and enterprise selling share the shelf with infrastructure on purpose, because shipping a system takes all of them.

About the curator

I’m Harsh Jha. I lead AI engineering at Formee Holdings in Bengaluru, where we build generative AI, RAG and agentic systems that have to hold up in production.

I came to AI from backend engineering. At SimplyPhi I shipped more than twenty production features and fifty REST APIs in Go and Python, on Azure and Oracle Cloud, and led a team of developers. Before that I studied at IIT Indore. I curate this library with a team of fellow IITians.
